## Full description (4000 max)

Breath keeps a quiet count of the breaths left in your day. When someone crosses your mind, hold their name, and one of those breaths goes to them. They simply know you were thinking of them. No messages to write. No reply expected.

AN OLD IDEA, GENTLY
An old teaching says every being is born with a fixed number of breaths. A life is measured not in years, but in breaths. Breath borrows that attention and turns it outward, towards the people you love. Read the yogic, Buddhist and Tibetan ideas behind it, with the original Sanskrit, Pāli and Tibetan, inside the app.

HOW IT WORKS
• Invite your people with a link over WhatsApp, SMS or anywhere. No phone numbers, no contact uploads.
• Hold a name. A ring fills and a heartbeat crosses the screen. Let go to cancel.
• Each gift costs one of your own breaths, which is what makes it sincere.
• They see that you spent a breath on them. They can smile, or breathe one back.

YOUR MONTH, TOGETHER
Tap a name to see this month's breaths between you as a quiet grid of days: the days you gave, the days they gave, the days you both did. Only on your phone. No streaks, no scores.

BREATH FOR FOCUS
Price a task in breaths before you begin — 450 breaths is about half an hour — and watch them pass on a calm breathing dial. Your breaths don't pause, and neither does a focus session. In the evening, see where today's breaths went.

A GENTLE MORNING
Turn on Morning breath for one quiet nudge a day: "Who's on your mind today?" Choose what today's breaths are for.

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Add the Breath widget: the day as a breathing dial of quarter-hour dots, with your pinned people a tap away.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No feeds, likes, streaks or ads.
• Just a name: no email, phone number or password.
• Block anyone, and they won't be told.
• Delete your account and everything with it, any time, in Settings.

The count is a contemplative estimate — about fifteen breaths a minute until midnight — not a medical measurement or a prediction. It is a device for attention.
