Breath

Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Breath ("the app") is a contemplative app that shows an estimated count of breaths remaining in the day and lets you "gift a breath" to people you are connected with.

What we collect, and why

DataWhyShared with
The name you type inShown to the people you connect with and gift breaths toOnly people you are connected with
An anonymous account IDCreated automatically so the app knows which gifts are yours. No email, phone number or password is involvedNo one
Your invite codeLets someone connect with you when you share itAnyone you give it to
Your name on your invite linkSo the invite page can say "Asha would like to give you a breath"Anyone who has your invite link or code
Your connections (names of people you connected with, and whether you blocked them)To show your list of people and enforce blockingNo one
Gifts you receive (who sent it and when)To show you the giftNo one
A notification address for your phone (only if you allow notifications)So the people you're connected with can let your phone know when they give you a breathSent through Expo's push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which deliver the notification

This data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Singapore region), which Breath uses as its server. It is encrypted in transit. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with any other party.

On the website, Breath uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts.

Breath does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

The app also saves a few settings on your device only: how many breaths you have gifted today, the people you have pinned, when you last gave each person a breath, and whether you have seen the introduction. These never leave your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app.

When you choose "share image", the app creates a picture of your card and hands it to the app you pick in the share sheet. Breath does not keep or upload it.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → delete my account. This permanently deletes your name, code, invite link name, connections and received gifts from Breath's server, and your anonymous account. Details and other options: account deletion.

Children

Breath is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/aviratna/breath-app/issues

The count is a device for attention, not a medical or actuarial prediction.
