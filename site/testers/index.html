Breath on Google Play

Help Breath take its first breath.

Before a new app can appear on Google Play, Google asks for at least twelve people to test it for fourteen days. If you have an Android phone, you can be one of them. It takes two minutes.

1. Send us your Google Play email

The Gmail address you use on your Android phone. We add it to the test list, usually within a day.

Email ikistudio.app@gmail.com

2. Join the test

Once we tell you you’re on the list, open this link on your phone and tap Become a tester.

Join the Breath test on Google Play

3. Install Breath from Google Play

The same page links to the Play Store. If you installed Breath from our website before, uninstall that one first.

4. Keep it for fourteen days

Leave Breath installed and opted in for fourteen days in a row, and open it now and then: give a breath to someone, try a focus session. Google counts testers who stay.

Tell us what you notice, good or confusing, at ikistudio.app@gmail.com. That is the most helpful thing a tester can do.
