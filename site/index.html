Every being is born with a fixed number of breaths.

The Vedic and Tibetan Buddhist traditions counted a life not in years, but in breaths. To notice one passing is to notice a little of a life passing with it. Breath borrows that practice, gently.

How it works

Say it without saying anything.

01

Invite your people

Share a link over WhatsApp or anywhere. When they open it, you’re connected. No phone numbers, no contact uploads.

02

Hold a name

A ring fills as you hold. Let go to cancel. Each gift costs one of your own breaths, which is what makes it sincere.

03

They simply know

They see that you spent a breath on them. They can just smile, or breathe one back. There is no thread to keep up.

Just a name

No email, phone number or password. Only the people you connect with can see you.

Quiet by design

No feeds, likes, streaks or ads. Nothing asks for your attention except the people you love.

Yours to control

Block anyone, and they won’t be told. Delete your account and everything with it, any time. Privacy policy
