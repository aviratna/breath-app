# Breath — Delete your account and data

**App:** Breath (developer: aviratna)

## Delete it yourself in the app (immediate)

1. Open Breath.
2. Tap the **⚙** gear (top right) to open **Settings**.
3. Under **Account**, tap **delete my account**, then tap again to confirm.

## What is deleted

Everything Breath stores about you on its server, immediately and permanently:

- your name and invite code;
- your list of connections, and your entry in other people's lists;
- the gifts you have received;
- your phone's notification address;
- your anonymous account.

If someone had **blocked** you, their record of that block is kept, so the block
keeps working if you create a new account. It contains only the name you used.

Nothing is kept for backups beyond Google Firebase's standard operations.

## If you no longer have the app

Open an issue at https://github.com/aviratna/breath-app/issues titled
"Delete my Breath account", including your invite code (shown in the app under
Settings → You). Do not post anything else personal. Your data will be deleted
within 30 days.
